# Warren Hennon

Nutritional Therapy Practitioner and builder of AI-powered tools for health and wellness practitioners. I build with AI-assisted development and focus on software that helps people.

## What I've built

**Divergent Portal**: [divergentportal.com](https://divergentportal.com)
A practice-management platform for Nutritional Therapy Practitioners, currently in beta with practitioners from my NTP cohort. Its focus is reasoning intelligence: helping practitioners think through their clients' most difficult nutrition and lifestyle challenges.
*Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Clerk, Vercel, and the Anthropic Claude API.*

**Eunoia Presence**: [eunoiapresence.app](https://eunoiapresence.app)
An AI wellness companion app for judgment-free emotional reflection by text and voice.
*Built with React, Python FastAPI, Supabase, Stripe, ElevenLabs, and the Anthropic Claude API.*
